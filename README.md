# 🏡 Township 2D 🌾🐮🚂

> **Build. Farm. Produce. Trade. Grow your Town!**

**Township 2D** is a 2D farming and town-management simulation game where you take the role of the **Mayor**. Build your town, grow crops, feed cows, manufacture products, fulfill citizen orders, and manage cargo trains to collect construction materials and expand your town.

---

## 🎮 Game Overview

Your journey starts with a small town and a few farming plots.

As Mayor, you will:

* 🌾 Grow and harvest crops
* 🐮 Feed and milk cows
* 🏭 Manufacture useful products
* 🚁 Complete citizen orders
* 🚂 Manage cargo trains
* 📦 Manage Barn storage
* 🏗️ Build and upgrade your town
* ⭐ Earn Coins and XP
* 🧱 Collect construction materials
* 📈 Expand your town and unlock new buildings

The goal is simple:

**Produce → Fulfill Orders → Earn Rewards → Upgrade → Expand → Grow!**

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌾 Farming System

Grow different crops on your farm plots:

| Crop         | Use             |
| ------------ | --------------- |
| 🌾 Wheat     | Cow Feed, Bread |
| 🌽 Corn      | Corn Meal       |
| 🥕 Carrot    | Orders          |
| 🍭 Sugarcane | Sugar & Syrup   |

Crops have multiple growth stages, allowing you to watch your farm develop from small sprouts into fully grown crops.

### 🐮 Cow & Milk System

Take care of your cows by feeding them.

**Gameplay flow:**

```text
Cow Feed
   ↓
Feed Cow
   ↓
Eating / Digestion
   ↓
Milk Ready 🥛
   ↓
Collect Milk
```

Fed cows display an eating timer before becoming ready to produce milk.

## 🏭 Production System

Turn raw resources into valuable products using factories.

### 🌾 Feed Mill

| Product   | Ingredients | Time |
| --------- | ----------: | ---: |
| Cow Feed  |     2 Wheat |  10s |
| Corn Meal |      2 Corn |  20s |

### 🍞 Bakery

| Product |       Ingredients | Time |
| ------- | ----------------: | ---: |
| Bread   |           2 Wheat |  15s |
| Cake    | 2 Wheat + 1 Sugar |  45s |

### 🥛 Dairy Factory

| Product | Ingredients | Time |
| ------- | ----------: | ---: |
| Butter  |  1 Cow Feed |  30s |
| Cheese  | 1 Corn Meal |  40s |

### 🍬 Sugar Refinery

| Product | Ingredients | Time |
| ------- | ----------: | ---: |
| Sugar   | 2 Sugarcane |  25s |
| Syrup   | 3 Sugarcane |  50s |

When production is finished, collect the product from the **floating product bubble** above the factory.

## 🚁 Citizen Orders

The **Helipad** is one of the main ways to earn:

* 🪙 Coins
* ⭐ XP

### How it works

1. Open the Helipad.
2. Check the available customer orders.
3. Collect the required items.
4. Click **SEND**.
5. The helicopter leaves the town.
6. After **12 seconds**, it returns with your rewards.

### 💡 Hover Helper

Not sure how to produce an item?

Hover over the customer's name to see an animated guide showing how to obtain the required ingredients.

### 🗑️ Discard Orders

Don't want an order?

Click the **Trash 🗑️** button.

A replacement order will appear after approximately **20 seconds**.

# 🚂 Train Logistics

Your town has **three railway tracks**, each with a different cargo train.

### 🚅 Express Cargo Train

**Track 0**

* 📦 4 cargo crates
* ⚡ 12-second travel time
* Requires refined products
* Returns **double construction materials**

### 🚂 Local Freighter Train

**Track 1**

* 📦 3 cargo crates
* 🕐 25-second travel time
* Requires basic crops
* Returns standard construction materials

### 🚇 Metro Materials Train

**Track 2**

* 📦 5 cargo crates
* 🐌 45-second travel time
* Requires larger quantities
* Returns large shipments of construction materials

---

## 🔄 Recycle Cargo

Sometimes a train may request products that you cannot currently produce.

Use:

**🔄 Recycle Cargo**

to refresh the train's requests.

This gives you a chance to receive more manageable cargo requirements.

# 📦 Barn Storage

The **Barn** stores your:

* 🌾 Crops
* 🥕 Vegetables
* 🥛 Milk
* 🍞 Food
* 🧈 Manufactured products
* 🧱 Construction materials

Click the **📦 BARN** button to open your inventory.

### 🪙 Selling Items

If your Barn becomes full, sell unwanted items using the **Sell 🪙** button.

Selling products:

```text
Item
 ↓
Sell
 ↓
Earn Coins 🪙
 ↓
Free Storage 📦
```

## ⬆️ Barn Upgrade

Increase your storage capacity by collecting:

* 🧱 Bricks
* 🪨 Slabs
* 🥛 Glass

Then select **Upgrade Barn**.

Each upgrade increases storage capacity by:

### **+20 Slots 📦**

# 🏗️ Building & Shop System

Use the **Shop 🏗️** button to purchase new buildings and farm objects.

Possible buildings include:

* 🌾 Crop Plots
* 🐮 Cowshed
* 🌾 Feed Mill
* 🍞 Bakery
* 🥛 Dairy Factory
* 🍬 Sugar Refinery

### 🟢 Building Placement

When placing a building:

**Green = Valid location**

**Red = Invalid location**

Buildings cannot overlap existing structures or railway tracks.

Simply move the building to a valid green area and click to place it.

# 💡 Pro Tips

### 1. 🚂 Prioritize Trains

Cargo trains are your main source of construction materials.

Use them regularly if you want to upgrade your Barn.

### 2. 🐮 Keep Cows Fed

Milk is an important ingredient for your production chain.

Try to maintain a steady supply of Cow Feed.

### 3. 🚁 Complete Orders

Citizen orders provide Coins and XP, helping you progress faster.

### 4. 🔄 Recycle Difficult Cargo

If a train requests items you cannot produce, don't get stuck.

Use **Recycle Cargo** to refresh the requests.

### 5. 📦 Watch Barn Capacity

Don't allow your storage to remain full.

Sell unnecessary items when you need extra space.

### 6. 🏭 Plan Production

Start long production jobs early.

For example:

```text
Syrup → 50s
Cake → 45s
Cheese → 40s
Butter → 30s
Sugar → 25s
```

Use waiting time to farm, feed cows, or prepare orders.
